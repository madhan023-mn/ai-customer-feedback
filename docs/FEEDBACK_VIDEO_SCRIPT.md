## Zidio Development Capstone Project Reflection

> **Recommended Duration:** 60 to 90 seconds (1.0 to 1.5 minutes)  
> **Camera Setup:** Front-facing webcam/phone camera with clear audio and good lighting  
> **Submission Platforms:** YouTube (Set to **Unlisted**) or Google Drive (**Anyone with the link can view**)

### 📝 Spoken Script (Word-for-Word)

> *"Hello everyone and mentors at Zidio Development. My name is Madhan, and I am sharing my reflection on completing the Capstone Project — **Project LOOP: AI Customer-Feedback Intelligence Platform**.*
>
> ### 1. What I Learned
> *Throughout this internship, I gained deep practical experience in full-stack MERN architecture, multi-tenant database modeling in MongoDB, building Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines using vector embeddings, and creating responsive, interactive data visualizations with Recharts and React 18.*
>
> ### 2. Challenges Faced & Solutions
> *One major technical challenge I encountered was managing real-time AI classification for high-volume batch imports without blocking the event loop or failing in serverless environments. I designed a resilient architecture with BullMQ queues and an automatic synchronous fallback mechanism that guarantees continuous processing even when background workers are offline.*
>
> *Another challenge was eliminating hallucinations in executive reporting. I solved this by pre-aggregating statistics deterministically in Node.js and feeding verified numbers to the LLM solely for narrative synthesis.*
>
> ### 3. Key Takeaways & Gratitude
> *This capstone gave me hands-on engineering confidence to build secure, corporate-ready AI products from scratch. I want to express my sincere gratitude to the Zidio Development team and my mentors for their guidance and for providing such an industry-standard curriculum.*
>
> *Thank you!"*

### 💡 Tips for Recording:
1. **Pacing:** Speak at a calm, natural pace (approx. 130 words per minute).
2. **Audio:** Use earphones or a quiet room so your voice is crisp and audible.
3. **Framing:** Keep eye contact with the camera and maintain good posture.
